The problem: nobody can see inside a used battery

Buyers guess

A used pack looks the same whether it has 80% or 40% of its life left, so honest sellers get lowballed.

Lenders can’t price risk

Battery is the most expensive part of an electric vehicle, yet financing has no health data to rely on.

Good packs get scrapped

Healthy batteries are thrown away because no one can prove they still work, which wastes money and materials.

How PackPulse works

1

Clip the PackPulse module onto the battery. It measures voltage, current and temperature on every charge and ride.

2

The phone app turns those readings into a health score, remaining range and a resale grade.

3

Share a verified battery report with a buyer, a repair shop or a lender with one link.
